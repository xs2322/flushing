Restrooms

Every restroom the map knows about, open or not: the City's Public Restrooms list (the 17 Throne units included) and the MTA's subway station restrooms.

A restroom counts on the map when it is listed as open and its type is switched on. When it doesn't count, the last column says why: closed, planned, a subway restroom (off by default), or ruled out by a scenario you chose. More
