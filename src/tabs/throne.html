Throne pilot

The City's one-year Throne pilot ($4M, run by NYCEDC with DOT and Parks) began installing 17 free modular restrooms in September 2026, in 14 community districts. This page asks two things: does the map see need where the City put them, and how do those districts compare?

Units open 7 am–10 pm and lock after 10 minutes. They are unlocked by text message, QR code, the Throne app, or a tap card handed out by local partners. Advocates have asked how people without a phone will get in. Feedback on the pilot goes to 311.

Does the map agree with the City's sites?

The test leaves the 17 units out and looks at the blocks they stand on. The map's rules were not tuned to these sites, so agreement is a sense check, not proof.

The pilot is a one-year expense contract: $4M for 17 units, about $235,000 a unit on average (the City's report lists $270,000 on Staten Island). A second year is not yet funded (LL58 report, p. 18). Because units close at 10 pm, they count in the Evening scenario.
